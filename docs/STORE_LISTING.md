## 3. Full description (max 4000 characters)

### EN

```
Every walk is a chance to discover something real.

LifeDex turns the animals, plants, trees, and mushrooms around you into a
personal field guide. Point your camera at something alive, and LifeDex
identifies the species using real recognition technology — not a generic
"it's a bird" guess.

HOW IT WORKS
• Take a live photo of an animal, plant, tree, or mushroom.
• LifeDex identifies the species and shows you how confident the match is.
• Rarer finds earn more XP — a common pigeon won't beat a rare orchid.
• Every discovery becomes a collectible card with facts, stats, and a bit of
  natural history.

DISCOVER, DON'T DISTURB
LifeDex is built around one rule: looking shouldn't cost the animal or plant
anything.
• Sensitive and protected species (nesting birds, protected orchids, and
  more) get extra location privacy — their exact spot is never shown, even
  to you, on the map.
• Zoo and captive animals are recognized as such and capped at low XP, so
  there's no reason to farm rare species behind glass.
• Onboarding walks you through observing from a distance, staying on trails,
  and leaving protected plants where they are.

YOUR COLLECTION
Browse everything you've found by category or rarity, revisit any card to
re-read its story, and track your progress on a Stats & Achievements page —
streaks, milestones, and species counts, all just for you.

A MAP OF YOUR OWN DISCOVERIES
See where you've found things, with the same privacy rules as everywhere
else in the app: common species show an approximate area, and sensitive or
protected species are hidden behind a wide, fuzzy circle instead of a precise
pin.

PRIVACY, BY DESIGN
• Your original photo never leaves your device. Only a cropped, processed
  image is ever used for the card.
• Your exact location is never stored or shared — only an intentionally
  fuzzed, approximate one.
• This version of LifeDex has no accounts and no public feed. Nothing you
  capture is published or shown to other users.
• To identify a species, the photo is sent only to the iNaturalist Computer
  Vision API and/or Pl@ntNet — never your location, never anything else
  about you.
• Full details are in the in-app Privacy Policy, along with one-tap export
  and delete for all your local data.

ENGLISH AND GERMAN
LifeDex follows your device's language automatically and can be switched
anytime in Settings.

Free to play. No ads. No in-app purchases in this version.

LifeDex — discover, don't disturb.
```

## 4. Screenshots plan

Play requires at least 2 phone screenshots (JPEG/PNG, 16:9 or 9:16, min
edge 320px, max edge 3840px); Play recommends 4–8. The five below are the
requested core set, in the order they should appear (order matters — the
first 2–3 are what most users actually see before scrolling). Three optional
extras follow for a fuller listing. Capture on a real device or a
recognition-verified emulator (see `STATUS.md` — iNaturalist path
live-verified on Android), in **both** English and German device languages
(Settings → Language), producing two full image sets.

| # | Screen | What to show | EN caption overlay | DE caption overlay |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Capture** (`CaptureScreen`) | Live camera viewfinder aimed at a real plant/animal, shutter button visible | "Snap anything alive" | "Fotografiere alles Lebendige" |
| 2 | **Result / card** (`ResultScreen`) | The just-identified species result with confidence, rarity, and XP gained | "Instant AI species ID" | "Sofortige KI-Arterkennung" |
| 3 | **Collection** (`CollectionScreen`) | Grid of several collected cards, mixed categories/rarities, so it reads as a rich collection (not a near-empty one) | "Build your collection" | "Baue deine Sammlung auf" |
| 4 | **Card detail with lore** (`CardDetailScreen`) | A card detail view scrolled to the "About" section showing the Wikipedia-sourced species blurb | "Learn the story behind every find" | "Erfahre die Geschichte hinter jedem Fund" |
| 5 | **Stats / Achievements** (`StatsScreen`) | Profile hero (level ring) + achievements grid with a few unlocked | "Track your progress" | "Verfolge deinen Fortschritt" |
| 6 *(optional)* | **Map** (`MapScreen`) | A few pins/clusters plus at least one fuzzed privacy circle visible, to show off the privacy story visually | "Your discoveries, mapped — privately" | "Deine Funde auf der Karte — privat" |
| 7 *(optional)* | **Onboarding rule screen** (`OnboardingScreen`) | One of the three "Discover, don't disturb" rule cards | "Discover, don't disturb" | "Entdecken, nicht stören" |
| 8 *(optional)* | **Home** (`HomeScreen`) | Home dashboard with recent discoveries strip | "Your nature hub" | "Dein Naturzentrum" |

Notes:
- Before shooting screenshot 3 (Collection) and 5 (Stats), capture a handful
  of varied real sightings first (different categories/rarities) so the
  screenshots don't look sparse — a fresh install's empty state is not a good
  first impression.
- Don't stage a fake "legendary" find using `mockSpecies` hints in a
  production build — capture genuine finds so the screenshots reflect a real
  session, consistent with the "no invented claims" rule for this listing.
- Keep status bar / notification content clean (airplane mode or a clean
  time) when capturing, standard store-asset practice.
- If native `react-native-maps` isn't wired up yet at capture time (see
  `STATUS.md` — gated behind a dev build + Maps key), either wait until it is,
  or caption screenshot 6 generically enough that it stays accurate either
  way (the wording above works for both the stylised and native map).

## 5. Play Console "Data safety" section — guidance

This maps the app's actual behavior (as verified in the codebase, see the
"what was checked" list at the end) onto Play's Data safety questionnaire
categories. **This is guidance, not a substitute for completing the live
Play Console form** — Google's exact categories/wording change over time, so
re-check against the current form when you fill it in.

| Play category | Collected? | Shared with 3rd party? | Notes to enter in the form |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Photos** | Yes | Yes — iNaturalist, Pl@ntNet, Google Cloud Vision (whichever are configured) | Purpose: **App functionality** (species identification, content moderation). Not used for advertising or account management. Not processed ephemerally on our own server — we don't have one in the loop; it goes straight to the recognition provider (or via our relay, which doesn't retain it). Required for the app's core feature (capturing a sighting); the app can still be opened/browsed without granting camera access. |
| **Approximate location** / **Precise location** | Judgment call — see below | No | The device's precise location is read locally, used in memory to compute a fuzzed value, and discarded; it is never transmitted off the device. The fuzzed value is stored **locally only**. Under Play's definition ("collected" = transmitted off the device), a defensible answer is **"Data isn't collected."** If you'd rather be conservative, declare Location as collected-but-not-shared, purpose "App functionality," and add a note that only a fuzzed value is retained and solely on-device. |
| **Personal info** (name, email, user IDs, etc.) | No | No | No accounts, no sign-up, nothing to collect. |
| **App activity** | No | No | No analytics/telemetry SDK is present. |
| **App info and performance** (crash logs, diagnostics) | No | No | No crash-reporting SDK is present yet. **Revisit this row before release if Sentry (or similar) is added** — see `STATUS.md` step 6, which lists Sentry as a planned pre-launch item. |
| **Device or other IDs** | No (in default config) | No | LifeDex itself assigns no device/advertising ID. If a future build ships with Supabase configured and enabled, anonymous-auth would generate a random session ID — not applicable to the current default (Supabase off; see `SECURITY_AND_PRIVACY.md` / `STATUS.md`). |

**Data deletion:** Play now expects apps to explain how users can delete
their data even without a formal account. LifeDex can point to the in-app,
self-service flow: **Settings → Privacy & data → Delete all my data** —
immediate, on-device, no waiting period, no email required. Mention this
explicitly in the Play Console "data deletion" prompt instead of (or in
addition to) a web form.

**Encryption in transit:** all outbound calls (iNaturalist, Pl@ntNet, Google
Cloud Vision, Wikipedia) use HTTPS — you can answer "Yes" to the in-transit
encryption question.

**Ads:** none in this version — declare "No ads."

**Data collected only for security/fraud prevention, or required, etc.:**
not applicable at this stage (no auth system live in default config).

### What was checked to build this table
`src/config/features.ts` (`communitySharing: false`), `src/lib/supabase.ts`
(client is `null` unless URL+key configured), `src/lib/community.ts`
(`pushSighting`/`ensureAnonSession` are no-ops when Supabase is `null`, and
`pushSighting` is additionally gated by the feature flag), `src/services/
sightingPipeline.ts` (confirms the community push call site is behind the
flag), `src/domain/types.ts` (`Sighting.publicLocation` is the only location
field persisted — already fuzzed), `src/providers/inaturalist/inatClient.ts`,
`src/providers/plantnet/plantnetClient.ts`, `src/providers/google/
visionClient.ts` (all three: photo only in the request body, no lat/lng, no
device or user identifiers), `src/lib/lore.ts` (Wikipedia call carries only
the species name), `src/screens/SettingsScreen.tsx` (real export/delete
implementation), `package.json` (no ad/analytics/crash SDK dependency
present).

## 6. Content rating

Not answerable from the codebase alone — Play's IARC questionnaire is filled
in live in Play Console and determines the actual rating. Based on what the
app contains (no user-generated public content in v1, no violence, no
gambling, no user-to-user chat), it's likely to land in the **"Everyone" /
PEGI 3** bucket, but this is the owner's call to make by actually completing
the questionnaire — don't hardcode an assumed rating into any marketing
material.

## 7. Target audience & Play's "Ads" declaration

- **Contains ads:** No (confirmed — no ad SDK in `package.json`).
- **In-app purchases:** No, not in this version (`STATUS.md`: monetization
  "not built").
- **Target age group:** Owner decision in Play Console; nothing in the app
  is specifically designed for children, and there's no dedicated
  under-13/under-16 experience or COPPA-style consent flow, so this is a
  straightforward "general audience" declaration unless the owner intends
  otherwise.
